# The advice corpus, as a negative result

**Date:** 2026-10-31
**Study:** `advice-corpus`, prompt `advice-v2`. 407 eligible posts about money addressed to founders, from ten firms' archives, January 2012 to September 2026.
**Registration:** `advice-corpus-preregistration.md` · **Full results, with every interval:** `advice-corpus-results.md`

## The result

Investor advice did not turn conservative when money got expensive.

The study registered the prediction that it would. When funding pressure crossed its line in July 2022, expansion advice should have become rarer and conservation advice commoner. Neither happened. Across seven firms with posts on both sides of the line, the share of posts telling founders to expand moved **+9.6 points** (95% CI −3.6 to +34.7), the wrong sign. Conservation advice fell at every lag from 0 to 12 months, from −7.3 points at lag 3 to −39.2 at lag 10.

By era, firm-weighted: below the line, 29.7% of posts told founders to expand and 47.2% to conserve. Above it, 39.2% and 40.0%.

## Why it matters

The genre did not re-write its stance. What moved was the scoreboard. Burn multiple appears in the corpus from 2022 (0% before, 3–8% after), CAC payback peaks the same year at 19%, and the Rule of 40 surfaces in 2024. Ten firms kept saying "grow" and changed the metric they said it with.

That is the gap this product is built on. A weekly brief that states a condition, an action, a threshold and a reversal is a different artifact from a stance essay, because it can be checked next month. The corpus is evidence that the incumbent genre does not track conditions closely enough to be checked that way.

## What would change this

- A second crossing with more posts after it. One crossing is one event, and 2022 also carried the fastest rate rise in four decades and the start of the AI funding cycle.
- Labels that clear the study's agreement bar. The local model agrees with a careful reader at kappa 0.52 against a registered 0.6, and its expand pile is about one-third expansion advice. A ten-point shift could hide in that noise.
- More firms above the line. The high-pressure era has a third as many posts as the decade before it, and two fewer firms.

The corpus is dated. A later run can answer all three.

## What this does not say

It does not say advice is immune to conditions. It says the association the study predicted is not visible in this sample, at this size, with these labels. It does not say which score matters: net stance correlated +0.22 with funding pressure at lag 0 (the wrong direction) and −0.05 with risk appetite. And it does not say why any author wrote what they wrote; a firm's blog is also its marketing.

Method, exclusions, the prefilter's recall and every robustness check are in the full results.
