# Surface brief: the site frame and This week (`/`)

Scope: every route's frame (navigation, record band, document) and the homepage first screen. Visitor mode: Read. The full design brief is `docs/design/rebuild-covenant.md`; this file holds the build contract only.

Audience and job: a founder reading on a Monday with a decision in flight; peers arriving cold (engineers, investors, designers). First screen must hold this week's guidance, whether anything moved, five areas with action and line, both scores.

Owner decisions (2026-11-10): one PR for the whole site; vector band with a month readout on every route plus a canvas rendering on History; History states the two differing records as facts with no cause; theme follows the device setting unless a choice is stored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The weekly brief is the dated statement of where each line stands, on paper whose only ornament is the record. It refuses the dark data panel with glowing accents and the cream editorial page with an italic serif masthead.

OWN-WORLD: Pale green-grey paper (`#ecf0e8`), green-black ink (`#10201a`), four condition inks (gold, sienna, periwinkle, deep blue), one red for a crossed line. Besley sets guidance, condition word, area names, score numerals and titles; Archivo sets everything that explains. Hairline rules and numbered sections, no cards. A full-bleed engraved band: condition fields crossed by two bundles of fine lines whose pattern changes with the condition.

STORY: The visitor sees how long the record is before reading, reads this week's guidance and where each of five areas stands, checks the two scores against their lines, and copies or subscribes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navigation, then the band (150px desktop, 84px phone) drawn from every month-end since Jan 2012 plus this week. Left 800px column: brief number and week, condition word with gloss and delta, guidance as the `h1` in Besley (51px desktop, 29px phone), facts line, five numbered area rows, copy and subscribe. Right 384px margin: funding pressure and risk appetite as 92px numerals with labelled ladders, trust facts, contents. Phone: one column, scores side by side under the areas.

FORM: Credit agreement and security printing (Covenant), third of seven grounded candidates; seed key 2b9b0296. Signature interaction: moving along the band reads out that month. Motion grammar: nothing on a quiet week; on a week that moved, the band gains its slot, the changed standing is struck and re-set, the score counts from last week.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
