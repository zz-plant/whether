# Surface brief: the site frame and This week (`/`)

Scope: every route's frame (navigation, record band, document) and the homepage first screen. Visitor mode: Read. The full design brief is `docs/design/rebuild-covenant.md`; this file holds the build contract only.

Audience and job: a founder reading on a Monday with a decision in flight; peers arriving cold (engineers, investors, designers). First screen must hold this week's guidance, whether anything moved, five areas with action and line, both scores.

Owner decisions (2026-11-10): one PR for the whole site; vector band with a month readout on every route plus a canvas rendering on History; History states the two differing records as facts with no cause; theme follows the device setting unless a choice is stored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The weekly brief is the week's weather report for a startup: the condition is the sky, and how to proceed follows from it. The page reads like a modern weather app — glanceable, condition-driven, calm — structured as a filed brief an investor could forward. It refuses the credit-agreement document and the editorial broadsheet alike.

OWN-WORLD: A condition-tinted sky (Sunny warm gold-white, Cloudy overcast grey-blue, Stormy deep slate, Freezing icy pale) as the call's contained block over a light paper body; the condition is the call's ground, not just a word, so a storm week and a sunny week look like different apps. Two voices: **Bricolage Grotesque** sets the display (guidance, condition word, score figures, wordmark) with its distinctive, slightly quirky letterforms that avoid the geometric-sans monoculture; **Source Sans 3** sets everything that explains (body, labels, area names) in a clean professional grotesque. Type roles: display (800, 28-40px, tracking -.02em, line-height 1.05); figure (800-900, 52-64px, tabular lining, tracking -.04em); heading (600-700, 16-20px); body (400, 15px, 1.6 line-height, 64ch measure); label (600, 11px, uppercase, .12em tracking); area (600, 16px). Hairlines and section rules; elevated surfaces (cards, buttons) carry a layered multi-shadow (three layers, Stripe/Linear style) for physical depth. Metric rows like an investor update. The record band becomes a weather strip: condition fields as sky, scores as large numerals. A condition glyph (sun/cloud/storm/snow) beside the word, so colour is never the only carrier. Controls, links and focus rings stay in the ink in every week. No glow halos (AI-pattern tell); no zero-offset shadows. Layering: the sky carries atmospheric depth (soft animated cloud masses at different positions, slow drift), while the body stays flat and readable.

STORY: The visitor glances at the sky and knows the week's weather; reads the guidance; checks the five areas and two scores; copies or shares. The condition drives the whole page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One reading column and one evidence rail. The call sits on its condition in a contained sky block (rounded, condition-tinted): the week line, condition glyph + word, guidance as the `h1` in display weight, and the week's changed line in the crossed ink. Beneath it: five ruled area rows, each with its standing, action and canonical line; then the calibration and copy actions. The rail carries both scores as figures, "Why this call", "History" and the trust facts. Phone: the sky block stacks, the two compact score figures follow the guidance on a panel of paper, and the area rows keep their action and line. The record band above the page is one closed summary line carrying the category line.

FORM: User-pinned hybrid of filed brief (Data Room) + investor-update letter + modern weather-app condition theming (one-page brief, declutter round seed `23003e23`, approved option 3). Signature interaction: moving along the weather strip reads out that month's condition and scores, and the page's sky tints to that month. Motion grammar: the sky cross-fades on a condition change (300ms); area rows tick on a week that moved; reduced motion respected.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
