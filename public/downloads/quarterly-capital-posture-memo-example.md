# Quarterly Capital Discipline Memo (Example)

> **This is a worked example, not a live reading.** The figures below are from an
> illustrative quarter and were never a published reading. For this week's
> guidance, the room before a change and the conditions that would change it, see
> https://whether.work — and for the blank version of this document,
> /downloads/capital-posture-template.md.

**Quarter:** Q3 (Jul–Sep) · **Prepared for:** Board meeting, 8 Oct
**Conditions at quarter end:** Freezing · **Room before a change:** within reach of the line
**Source reading:** funding pressure 74/100 (line: 70) · risk appetite 41/100 (line: 50)

## Executive summary

- **Conditions this quarter:** Freezing, and they held all thirteen weeks. We entered
  the quarter one week after conditions moved off Sunny and we have not been
  back.
- **Why now:** Funding pressure sat 4 points above the line where conditions
  change for the whole quarter, driven by the front end rather than by spreads.
  Risk appetite stayed below its line on a flat-to-inverted curve. Neither score
  reversed, so nothing in the plan below was re-opened mid-quarter.
- **Key risks:** Two of our three largest commitments are annual and renew in
  Q4, so the cost of being wrong about the guidance rises in November, not now.

## Committed / Staged / Off the table

| Decision | Class | Owner | Reversal path | Status at quarter end |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backfill two departures in Support | Committed | VP Ops | Hiring freeze, 30-day notice | Done, both filled |
| Renew the data vendor at current seats | Committed | CFO | Month-to-month at +12% | Done |
| Open the Series B process | Staged | CEO | Withdraw before term sheet | **Deferred to Q4** |
| Hire 4 engineers against the FY plan | Staged | CTO | Offers rescinded pre-start | Cut to 2, both senior |
| Two-year compute reservation | Off the table | CTO | None — prepaid, non-refundable | **Not taken** |

The classification is the decision's reversibility, not its size. The compute
reservation was the cheapest line on this list per unit and the only one with no
way back, so it is the one we did not do.

## Pause and resume conditions

Each condition is a number, a duration, an owner, and what happens when it is
met. A condition without all four is a note, not a condition.

1. **Re-open the Series B process** — resume when funding pressure closes below
   52 for two consecutive weekly reads. Owner: CEO. On breach: re-open the
   process at the next Monday reading, no board vote required.
2. **Release the two held engineering reqs** — resume when risk appetite closes
   above 50 for two consecutive weekly reads *and* conditions are not Freezing.
   Owner: CTO. On breach: reqs go live within five working days.
3. **Pause all discretionary spend above $25k** — pause when funding pressure
   closes above 75, or conditions read Freezing in any single week. Owner: CFO.
   On breach: spend authority drops to the CFO for the rest of the quarter, and
   this memo is re-issued inside a week.

Conditions 1 and 2 were not met this quarter. Condition 3 came within 1 point of
being met.

## Board asks

- **Approvals requested:** Carry the two unfilled engineering reqs into Q4 at the
  same level rather than re-scoping them. Authorise the Series B process to open
  on condition 1 without a further board vote.
- **Guardrails requested:** Keep the $25k discretionary ceiling in condition 3 for
  Q4 even if conditions improve, because the annual renewals land inside it.
- **Next review:** Weekly against the conditions above; full re-issue of this memo
  at quarter end, or within a week of condition 3 being met.
